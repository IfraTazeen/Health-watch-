🔐 Your Privacy & Data
Before you start, here is exactly how your data is handled:
  • 📱
    Stays on your phone only — your health data never leaves your device. No servers. No cloud. Nobody else can see it.

    We collect nothing — no name, no email, no location. HealthWatch does not know you exist.

    You own your data — you can download it anytime from the Dashboard. You can delete it anytime by clearing browser data.
  • 👨‍👩‍👧
    Not shared with anyone — no advertisers, no third parties, ever.

      🟢 Monitoring
      Select your symptoms above
      Tap what you have been feeling and how long. We will tell you honestly what level of care you may need. 💙
        🚨 Go to hospital immediately if:
        • 🔴You black out or lose consciousness completely
        • 🔴Chest pain or extreme difficulty breathing
        • 🔴Severe headache that feels like the worst of your life
        • 🔴Vision goes completely dark or blurry suddenly
        • 🔴Heart racing so fast you feel you might faint
        • 🔴You feel something is seriously wrong — trust that feeling
        🩺 What to tell your doctor
        Come prepared. This makes your visit much more effective.
        • 1
          When symptoms started and how often they occur
        • 2
          Which activities trigger them — standing, eating, prayer, exercise
        • 3
          Your sleep pattern — when you sleep, how many hours
        • 4
          Your diet — what you eat, how much water you drink
        • 5
          Any medications you are currently taking
        • 6
          Show them your HealthWatch logs — real data helps doctors diagnose faster
        🧪 Tests to ask for
        These blood tests give the clearest picture:
        🩸 CBC (Complete Blood Count)
        🧬 Vitamin B12
        ☀️ Vitamin D
        🔩 Serum Iron & Ferritin
        🦴 Calcium
        🧲 Magnesium
        🩺 Thyroid (TSH)
        🍬 Blood Sugar
        🌿 While you wait for your appointment
        Small steps that support your body:
        • 💧
          Drink 8–10 glasses of water daily
        • 🍽️
          Eat iron-rich foods — lentils, spinach, eggs, beetroot with lemon
        • 🌞
          Get 10–15 minutes of morning sunlight
        • 🧘
          Rest when dizzy — don't push through it
        • 🚫
          Avoid white sugar and excess tea/coffee
        • 💊
          Take prescribed medications consistently
        • 🛑
          Stand up slowly — always hold something for support
        • 💙
          Tell someone in your family how you're feeling
        ⚕️ Medical Disclaimer: The alert levels shown are based on general patterns only. They are not a medical diagnosis. Always consult a qualified doctor for proper evaluation and treatment.
